\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%=======================================================================

This paper asked how a satellite-ground edge network should schedule status updates when what matters is whether the
edge estimate is correct, and when the satellite links that carry most of the traffic fail for a fraction of a second
at each handover and for a whole pass under blockage. We formulated the robust problem VANGUARD, reduced the
history-dependent AoII to a tabulated cost per inter-delivery interval, and solved the resulting minimax assignment
with SENTINEL, which dualizes the minimax into classically updated scenario weights, encodes each domain's linear
subproblem as a compact QUBO whose one-hot structure the XY mixer preserves, coordinates the domains by ADMM, and
certifies every schedule by its exact worst case.

Under Starlink passes and the TR 38.811 channel the certificate held in 98.7\% of epochs; the robust schedule's
certificate was 2 to 7\% below the nominal one while the two were indistinguishable in realized AoII with and without
failure feedback; and SENTINEL with a state-aware trigger came within 10 to 20\% of the best per-slot heuristic, which
coincides with the Whittle-index scheduler at these bandwidths, and beat it by 25 to 30\% at 15 to 20 UEs without
feedback. The compact formulation needs four to six times fewer qubits than a direct epigraph encoding, matches the
exact optimum on the 10- and 25-UE instances, and is limited at 75 to 100 UEs by the coordination of shared
satellites; thirteen device domains of 8 to 38 qubits decoded to the exact optimum, with the fraction of usable shots
falling from 86\% to 2.6\% and not improved by error suppression, and at these sizes the quantum sampler is correct
but not a better optimizer than greedy association. Three directions follow: a coordinator that negotiates per-UE
assignments rather than aggregate bandwidth, to remove the bottleneck above 50 UEs; a scenario set learned from the
pass statistics of the site; and whether a constrained quantum sampler concentrates on the optimal set at the
150-qubit scale where greedy association starts to fail.
